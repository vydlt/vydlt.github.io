Hey, welcome to my silly corner!

I intended to put this in the blog description, but it became too long. So here it is, as a short post instead. 

Let me yap about what I do here. I've created this space purely for my own interests, where I occasionally write about life, work, studies, and whatever else is on my mind. 

I want to say that I’m still finding my voice and would never consider myself a true writer. Honestly, I’ve been writing for years, but I still struggle with self-doubt and hardly ever feel good about what I write. This space is my commitment to actually write and keep writing. 

Although writing doesn't come easily to me, it brings clarity to my thoughts. It helps me reflect, better understand myself, and make sense of the world. 

If I keep hesitating, I may never end up writing anything at all. And if I never write, I’ll never have the chance to return to my thoughts and see how they've evolved. 

Therefore, I’ve decided to write anyway, even if it’s just a few bullet points or a handful of condensed keywords. 

More than anything, I want this to be a space for reflection, learning, and capturing thoughts that I may want to revisit later.

Oh, and it's also a good excuse to practice my English.

It might sound surprising, but I've written plenty of introductions before. This is the first one I've actually felt good about.

I think being able to write something that feels true to yourself, and that you're genuinely happy with, is pretty cool. It's something I'm still learning and trying to do.

Hopefully, I'll eventually get the hang of it. For now, I'm curious to see where this leads.

That's all. Thanks for coming by.
